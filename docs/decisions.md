## Phase 2 Safety, job level (Ethan, 30/10/2026)

Plan approved with these answers:
1. **Job-level PDFs go in the job folder** (`CBS System/Operations/Jobs/Job N/<folder>`, `taskFolder()`), not live's company `H&S/<type>` folders.
2. **Revisions:** each revision is its own file ("… rev 2.pdf"); the old one is marked Superseded.
3. **Signed copies** are re-saved automatically every time someone signs (`src/lib/signons/service.ts`). The SharePoint service ("CBS Hub - Files") never overwrites (it renames on a clash), so each re-save is a new file named with the count ("… - signed (3).pdf"), and the hub marks the earlier copy replaced; the earlier copies stay in SharePoint unless a delete step is added to that workflow (Ethan's call).
4. **"Can approve permits" tick** on People (`permit_approve` in `people.lists`), off by default; Ethan Paraha and Preview Admin on dev. `canApprovePermits()`.
5. **Crew pages** are built inside each PR (not one PR at the end).
6. **No CoSHH public share link.**
7. **Order:** Sign-ons, RAMS, Toolbox talks, Pre-starts, CPHSP, Permits, Inspections, Visitors, Incidents, Job CoSHH, CDM site file. A checklist for Ethan after every 2 or 3 merged PRs.
8. **Templates:** dev is seeded from the old builders' lists; live's versions are imported at switch-over.

- **Sign-ons** (`sign_ons`, migration `20261030160000_safety_sign_ons.sql`): one signing system for RAMS, pre-starts, toolbox talks and CPHSP inductions. Each person signs a document's current revision once (name picked: on site today from the ServiceM8 schedule first, then the crew list, or typed; CBS or another company; tick to accept; signature). "Next person" clears it for the next on the same device. A new revision needs everyone to sign again. Job > Sign-ons lists them, with Needs attention for ones not yet reviewed, Mark reviewed, and Remove (in-app are-you-sure; the signed copy is made again without them). Document types plug in through `src/lib/signons/docs.ts`.
- **Crew page** (`/crew/<job no>?t=<token>`): the job's onsite page, no sign-in, name picked per action, never remembered. The token is worked out from the job number and `TEAM_LINK_KEY`, so each job's link opens only that job and changing the key cuts off every link. The office copies it from Job > Sign-ons > More ▾ > Crew page link. Pointing the ServiceM8 Onsite button at it is a switch-over step (it now goes to the old hub's crew page).
- **RAMS** (`rams`, `rams_templates`, migration `20261030170000_safety_rams.sql`; Job > Safety > RAMS, `/jobs/<job>/rams`): the old RAMS builder's 12 sections, one screen per RAMS. **+ New ▾** > CBS RAMS (start from a job type, or blank) or Subcontractor RAMS. A CBS RAMS starts from its job type: the standard opening steps, the job type's steps, the standard closing steps, its hazards from the library, its PPE and permits. Steps are added, removed and moved; hazards come from the library (search, or suggested by the equipment categories ticked in section 6) or "+ Add hazard" (with "Also add to the hazards library"); assessed CoSHH substances are ticked. **Save draft**, then **Issue RAMS**: it needs the activity, description of works, a task step, a hazard, prepared by and approved by. Issuing builds the PDF in the house format and saves it as its own file, "Job N - RAMS N-seq rev R - <activity>.pdf", in `Job N/RAMS` (the test folder until switch-over); the revision before becomes **Superseded**. An issued revision can't be changed: **More ▾ > Create new revision** makes a draft copy (revision + 1, approved by cleared) that's issued and signed again. Only the current issued revision can be signed: "Sign on (this device)" in the office, or the crew page. **More ▾ > Save as job type** keeps the RAMS's task steps, library hazards, PPE and permits as a job type. Drafts can be deleted (in-app are-you-sure); issued ones stay on record. Needs attention: drafts not issued, issued RAMS whose library hazards changed since issue (may need a new revision), and subcontractor RAMS not approved. Locked at Estimate.
- **Subcontractor RAMS:** the subcontractor (from the register, or added from the dropdown by name), what it covers, Upload file (any type), then Approve or Reject (rejecting needs a note). A new upload sends it back to review. Not signed through the hub.
- **Templates** (Health and safety > Templates, `/safety/templates`): the RAMS job types tab now; toolbox talk topics and CPHSP wording are added as tabs with their builders. + New job type, open one to edit it in the RAMS builder (template mode: the task steps, hazards, PPE, permits, scope, plant and materials only; new hazards always go to the library, since job types name library hazards), rename, Retire (hidden from + New > CBS RAMS; RAMS already made from it don't change) and Bring back. Dev and local are seeded with the old builder's 12 job types (`scripts/seed/dev-rams-job-types.sql`); live's are imported at switch-over.
