## Page standard (Ethan, 29/10/2026; revised the same day from his wireframes and live screenshots)

Every page uses the shared parts; nobody builds their own. Layout follows Ethan's three wireframes; the look (colours, header, menu, pills, tables) follows the live hub.

- **Header** (`src/components/shell/page-header.tsx`): white; ‹ Back on the left (not on Home); the light "Search jobs, people, documents…" box (results under the box as you type); the orange + New task; the signed-in person's name pill (orange circle with initials, then their name), Sign out inside its menu. **Deliberately differs from live** (Ethan, 29/10/2026): the search box stretches from just after ‹ Back (live has it on the right next to + New task), and every page except Home shows ‹ Back (live shows the page title in the header on Actions and Jobs instead).
- **Menu** (`src/components/shell/nav.tsx`), as live: dark charcoal, the logo on a white strip with the orange line under it; Home, Actions (red count of every open task, To do + In progress, as live's "30 open"), Jobs at the top with icons; then the groups Purchasing, Health and safety, Assets, People and companies, Compliance under uppercase, letter-spaced grey headings that carry the group's icon, their items indented under the heading; Settings last (with its icon, straight after Compliance, not pinned to the bottom). Only pages that exist are listed (a group with none is left out). The active item is solid orange. Shared parts is not in the menu.
- **Page top** (`PageTop`, `src/components/ui/page-top.tsx`), one row: left, the title with "N records" under it in grey; right, in this order, Reports ▾ (Create report and any other reports), More ▾ only where a page has other actions, then one orange + New … ("+ New ▾" with a short list where a page adds several kinds of thing, "+ New vehicle" with no ▾ where it adds one). All three buttons are the same height and corner radius. Never more than one orange button on the page itself (the header's + New task aside). Nothing between the page top and the filter pills (tabs, where a page has them, sit under the title row).
- **Under the page top** (`src/components/ui/register.tsx`), in this order: filter pills with counts (`FilterPills`: rounded, selected pill charcoal, counts orange); one row with the search box (widest, magnifier icon) and any dropdown filters (`FilterRow`); the list as a white table card (`DataTable`: grey heading row with uppercase grey headings, the first column a bold name with an optional grey line under it, pale tinted status pills with dark text that fill their column via `StatusPill`, › at the end of each row).
- **Needs attention panel** (`RegisterLayout` and `NeedsAttentionPanel`, `src/components/ui/page-top.tsx`; replaces the earlier button and 560px drawer): about 320px wide, always open down the right of every register page, the same pattern as the job page's Tasks panel; the page top, pills, search and list sit to its left. Title "Needs attention (N)" with » to hide it to a slim « tab on the right edge; shown or hidden is remembered on that device. Items grouped Overdue, then Due soon, each with a pale tinted tag (red overdue, amber due soon), a name and a detail line; clicking one opens its record. With nothing due it stays and shows "All clear" and "Nothing overdue or due in the next 30 days", so the page never jumps. Under 1100px wide it tucks behind a "Needs attention N" button bottom right (red if anything is overdue, amber if only due soon, grey when clear) that opens it full screen; the page behind doesn't scroll. Pages pass in their own items.
- **Team page** (People and companies > Team, `/people`): moved from Settings > People. Tabs as filled buttons (selected charcoal): Team, Training and qualifications, Time off, Team requests (`TeamTabs`). Team tab: filters Current, Left, Hub sign-in, Crew, All; columns Name (email under it), Role, Mobile, Hub sign-in (Yes / No; whether a PIN is set shows in the person's drawer), Status; + New team member; the Needs attention panel (All clear until Registers adds training items). A Crew tick on each person sets the `crew` entry in `people.lists` that the team app and the training matrix's "Crew list only" filter use. A **Time off** tick (Ethan, 29/10/2026) sets the `time_off` entry in `people.lists`: only ticked people may see the Time off tab, including its notes and files, because it holds sick notes. Nobody is ticked by default; on the dev database Ethan Paraha and Preview Admin are ticked for testing. `canSeeTimeOff(personId)` in `src/app/(hub)/people/data.ts` is the check. Core builds the page and the Team tab; Registers fills Training and qualifications and Team requests.
- **Fleet** (the example for Registers): the page top and the Needs attention panel; + New vehicle only (vehicle checks, services and accidents are added from inside the vehicle, as live); Reports ▾ > Create report (choose all vehicles or only those needing attention, and which columns). The list keeps its vehicle rows.
- **The round dark button on the right edge of preview pages** is the Vercel Toolbar (preview deployments only; never on Production). It isn't part of the app.
