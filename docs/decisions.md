# Decisions log

Ethan's decisions that change the plan, newest first. The full detail sits in `docs/plan.md` (sections 3.5 and 3.6); this log says what changed and when.

## Page standard (Ethan, 29/10/2026)

Every page uses the shared parts; nobody builds their own.

- **Header** (`src/components/shell/page-header.tsx`): white; ‹ Back on the left (not on Home); a light "Search jobs, people, documents…" box (results under the box as you type); the orange + New task; the signed-in person's name pill with initials, Sign out inside its menu.
- **Menu** (`src/components/shell/nav.tsx`): the logo on a white strip at the top with the orange line under it; items with icons under uppercase group headings, as live: Home, Actions (red count of open tasks), Jobs; Purchasing; Health and safety; Assets; People and companies; Compliance; Settings at the bottom. Only pages that exist are listed (a group with none is left out). The active item is solid orange. Shared parts is not in the menu.
- **Page top** (`PageTop`, `src/components/ui/page-top.tsx`), every register, always in this order: left, the title with "N records" under it; right, (1) Needs attention with a count (red if anything is overdue, amber if only due soon, grey "All clear" if nothing), (2) Reports ▾ (Create report and any other reports), (3) More ▾ only where a page has other actions, (4) one orange + New … ("+ New ▾" with a short list where a page adds several kinds of thing, "+ New vehicle" with no ▾ where it adds one). Never more than one orange button on the page itself (the header's + New task aside).
- **Needs attention drawer**: the same 560px right-hand drawer as tasks; items grouped Overdue, then Due soon, each with a coloured tag, a name and a detail line; clicking one opens its record; the page behind doesn't scroll. Pages pass in their own items. No due/attention panels at the top of pages: the list starts straight under the filters.
- **Under the page top** (`src/components/ui/register.tsx`): filter pills with counts (`FilterPills`: selected pill charcoal, counts orange); one row with the search and any dropdown filters (`FilterRow`); the list as a table (`DataTable`: white card, uppercase grey headings, filled status pills green / amber / red / grey / blue via `StatusPill`, › at the end of each row).
- **Team page** (People and companies > Team, `/people`): moved from Settings > People. Tabs Team, Training and qualifications, Time off, Team requests (`TeamTabs`). Team tab: filters Current, Left, Hub sign-in, Crew, All; columns Name, Role, Mobile, Hub sign-in, Status; a Crew tick on each person sets the `crew` entry in `people.lists` that the team app and the training matrix's "Crew list only" filter use. Core builds the page and the Team tab; Registers fills Training and qualifications and Team requests.
- **The round dark button on the right edge of preview pages** is the Vercel Toolbar (preview deployments only; never on Production). It isn't part of the app.

## 29/10/2026 (Phase 1 test round)

- **No custom requirement types.** Requirement types are built in only, defined in `src/lib/requirements.ts`. The Requirement types settings page, its menu item and the `requirement_types` table are removed (migration `20261029150000_drop_custom_requirement_types.sql`). Replaces plan 3.5 point 1 ("both"). Plan 3.6 point 13.
- **Site survey** is offered under Office tasks as well as On site tasks in + New task. Plan 3.6 point 14.
- **Search shows results as you type** in a list under the box; click a result to open it. Global search finds jobs (number, description, client), tasks and people; the Jobs page search works the same way for jobs. Plan 3.6 point 15.
- **One task panel, shown two ways** (replaces the earlier "drawer everywhere" line, 29/10/2026): a task opened from the job page (its Tasks panel, or the job's + New task) opens as a centred box over a blurred, dimmed page, as on the live Job File. Opened anywhere else (Actions board, search results, Home) it is the old hub's 560px right-hand drawer (`#ctd`), full height. Same panel, same Save, either way; the page behind never scrolls.
- **Job page + New task** matches the live Job File (Task Types, Stage Pick, Site Pick and Multi Pick in CBS - Job File): a centred box (620px, at most 92% of the screen high, only the box scrolls) over a blurred, dimmed page; subtitle "Job N. Tick everything this job needs, then Add."; Office tasks / On site tasks; Other · any one-off task at the top (On site task on the On site side), opening the one-off task form on the job; tiles grouped Estimate, Approved: Planning, Approved: on site, Completed, with Now on the job's current stage; tick several tiles (✓ Selected), then one Add tasks button (faded until something is ticked) next to Cancel. Tiles already on the job show On or Done and open their task; one-per-job and locked rules stay. No Category filter.
- **Header + New task** opens the generic task form (Link to Nothing or a job; the job pre-set on a job's pages), as the old hub's header button did. The requirement picker is the job Tasks panel's own + New task.
- **Job page:** the Tasks panel sits down the right (380px), as in the old Job File; on narrower screens it sits behind a Tasks button and opens full screen.
